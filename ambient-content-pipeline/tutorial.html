Multi-color Gradients

3 colors (40° hue steps)
acp run-all ... --gradient-direction diagonal_tl_br --gradient-colors "#4A90E2,#CE27E6,#E64E27"
4 colors with custom stops [0.0, 0.3, 0.7, 1.0]
acp run-all ... --gradient-direction diagonal_tl_br --gradient-colors "#FF6B6B,#4ECDC4,#45B7D1,#96CEB4" --gradient-stops "0.0,0.3,0.7,1.0"
Companion color: When --gradient-colors is not set, the pipeline auto-generates a companion by rotating the background hue 40°. For the default blue (#4A90E2), that produces #4E27E6. Use --gradient-stops to control where each color sits (0.0–1.0). Without stops, they're evenly distributed.

Animation parameters: --anim-intensity (0.0–0.5, default 0.2) controls how much movement. --anim-speed (0.1–2.0, default 1.0) controls rate. --anim-loop (seconds, default 60) sets clip duration. --anim-seed makes animations deterministic for reproducibility.

Important: Using --animate automatically enables --audio and --bg-music. The pipeline generates TTS narration and background music for each post, then mixes them together.
06

Audio: TTS & Background Music

Text-to-Speech (TTS)

Powered by edge-tts (Microsoft Edge TTS). The --audio flag generates an MP3 narration of the post text. Choose a voice with --audio-voice (default: en-US-AriaNeural). Other options: en-US-GuyNeural, en-US-JennyNeural, en-GB-SoniaNeural.
♫
TTS Narration
TTS: en-US-AriaNeural reading sample text

Background Music

Powered by ACE-Step AI music generation. The --bg-music flag generates instrumental background music. Use --bg-music-prompt to describe the mood, instruments, tempo, and atmosphere. If not provided, the pipeline auto-generates a prompt from the monthly theme via Ollama. Music is generated for the full month and reused per post.
♫
Background Music
BG Music: prompt='Serene, uplifting orchestral piece with strings and piano, slow tempo, evoking peace and divine grace, perfectly capturing faith, hope, and love without vocals or percussion.'

Mixed Audio (TTS + Music)

The final audio mix places TTS at volume 1.0 with a 0.5s delay, then layers background music at volume 0.3. Output is mono 44100Hz. The music track is trimmed to the slot video duration (TTS duration + 25% tail, max 3.0s).

Post-generation tools:
  • acp preview — HTML preview of all generated text for the month
  • acp regen-text --dates 2026-05-04,2026-05-05 — regenerate specific dates
  • acp rerender --date 2026-05-04 --texture-type grain_film — re-render with new visual settings
  • acp refine-posts --feedback "2026-05-04::Make it more direct" — AI-assisted iterative refinement
