Severity breakdown: Critical: 3 Major: 20 Moderate: 9

Key finding: Most problems occur in spoken-word/narrative sections with small base font sizes (72–76 px). The renderer scales text down to as little as 49% of base size to fit long lines, and several lines overflow the frame edges. Multi-line layouts via words_overrides restore full font size and keep text within the safe area.

Pattern Analysis

  • Most affected artifacts: where-ben-has-been, the-hidden-library, woe-to-you, blessed-the-name, and what-is-truth account for the majority of severe long-line cases.
  • Common font size: Nearly all problematic lines use font_size: 72 (intro/spoken-word preset). At this size, lines over ~50 characters trigger scaling; lines over ~75 characters often overflow.
  • Line type: Spoken-word, narrative, and repetitive-vamp lines dominate the list. Dense rap or chant passages also appear.
  • Audit correlation: All 7 audit overflow warnings at 960×540 fall inside this candidate set, confirming the measurement-based detection.
  • Renderer note: Styled-text sections (e.g., text_style: "neon") render word images with minimal horizontal gap. The multi-line split still solves the scaling/overflow problem, but a separate renderer word-spacing adjustment may further improve readability in those styles.
