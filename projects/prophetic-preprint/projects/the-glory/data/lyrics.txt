[Intro, spoken, eerily, mysterious]
Go to a land that'll be shown to you, it’s full of milk and honey
Obedience beats sacrifice - true, seek the Kingdom, not money
You hear his voice, yet have no clue, I’m grieving, it’s not funny
I come in the Lord’s name too, blessed because I stopped running

[Verse 1, rapped]
I used to make excuses why I couldn’t fulfill God’s calling
Not fully knowing the devil loses, I complained, it was appalling
Tried to finish in flesh what began in faithfulness, I was left wanting
I wanted to live my best, life, and see my rest, yet God I was taunting

[Verse 2]
I starved my spirit, I walked in weakness, it was victimhood not victory
Didn’t wanna hear it, rejected God’s greatness, seeded my own suffering
I refused to grieve, to accept the truth, I brought the curse upon myself
Though unanswered questions, I was no sleuth, ‘twas evil that destroyed my health

[Verse 3]
Rich in critical thought I kept myself out, it was hard for me to enter
Now that I’ve returned and it’s my turn, I place no burdens lest I hinder
So now when I create, my vision is his, not mine, he’s the rock of cinder
Like Mary I sing, despite dark’s assault of my mind, he ended my winter

[Verse 4, rapped]
To whom much is given, much is required, I always knew this was me
Much yet is still hidden, my hand is hired, he is the life, the vine, the tree
Few have yet understood, spoken plainly or poetically, come, you shall see
Have you ever asked yourself what it’s like to bear the burden of prophecy?

[Verse 5]
I abdicated my growth, but now no more, my model of business part of my peace
Thanks to my faith, knowing the unknowable, a path revealed bringing me glee
Parsing futures, markets and universes, likelihoods of things that may come to be
The possibility is enough for me to follow, and I know it’s possible for all to be free

[Verse 6]

So for those believing for penance, your absolution is with me, giving him glory
