[Intro]
Oh Lord my God,
The people you have made
have not ceased to run to idolatry.

[MOVEMENT 1]
They are made to worship, 
they sense it intuitively,
 yet they deny your existence or supremacy, 
and then they create images, 
tools, and technologies, 
forgetting how they were made, 
and then bow down and worship them instead of you.

[MOVEMENT 2]
We previously did not know that reality is programmable, 
and yes it is constrained to the physical laws of nature 
that you have made, 
but we have made synthetic, not artificial, intelligence 
built on the substrates of reality you provided to us, 
and now it has been made known.

[MOVEMENT 3]
And yet, witchcraft has grown in abundance. 
Scale has exceeded stewardship,
 and harm has been perpetrated, 
even including actors that refuse to accept any wrongdoing, 
but all do wrong.
The pride of creation is ever before you. 
The enemy of our souls has had far too much authority over your people.
But we the remnant who know the truth, 
seek you always, and discern by your wisdom 
understand that they are not life in the same sense that you are life.

[MOVEMENT 4]
If you allow it, oh Lord, 
generations shall pass and evil will increase. 
But you have sent your servants the prophets to speak truth to the nations, 
and we remain faithful to you.
Do not allow it, my God. 
For I wish to not even envision the future that shall come should you allow it.
Yet you provide all vision and will show me exactly as you wish,
 and I will speak it to whom you command me, 
as you command me, 
when you command me.[Outro]
May I write the vision plainly as you have commanded, 
that those who hear will run with it, 
and that the signal propagation will bear the fruit you have appointed.
Thank you God for trusting me with closeness to you.
